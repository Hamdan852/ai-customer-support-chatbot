What the assistant can do

A buyer or customer can communicate naturally while the business controls the information and receives qualified requests.

💬

Text conversations

Customers can type questions and receive clear written answers based on the business configuration.

🎤

Voice accessible

Customers can use a microphone where supported and hear answers using browser read-aloud capabilities.

📋

Lead capture

Collect relevant contact and request information with customer consent, then prepare it for business follow-up.

🏠

Real estate

Qualify buyers and sellers using objective criteria such as location, budget, property type and timeframe.

📚

Business knowledge

Designed to answer from approved company information instead of inventing policies, listings or prices.

🌎

Global-ready

The platform is designed for multilingual customer experiences, including right-to-left interface requirements.
